# Bob mockup generation prompts

Tool: built-in image generation.
All outputs are concepts for visual review.

## Original Bob pose study

Use case: style-transfer.
Asset type: character pose study for a native iPhone app, a landscape sheet containing three separate illustrations of the SAME existing mascot Bob.
Input image 1 is the identity reference and restyling target.
The user loves Bob's design exactly as it is.
Preserve the very specific original creature: broad irregular pear-shaped sage/olive green body, two floppy crooked ear stalks, sleepy asymmetrical large eyes with heavy eyelids, tiny curved smile with ONE off-center cream tooth, pale belly, two short squat feet and the small low appendage visible between them, scattered spots and small tufts of moss.
Preserve proportions, face spacing and lovable slightly dopey personality.
There are NO arms or hands.
No extra ears, no new anatomy, no clothing, accessories or props.
Primary request: reinterpret the reference as beautifully drawn 2D character art with the appeal of a polished 2010 Flash animation.
Solid muted green color areas, an economical dark olive contour, crisp hand-shaped edges, one hard-edged cel shadow plus a small restrained flat highlight per form.
Moss is a few designed flat shape clusters, spots are sparse flat marks.
Remove all fur fibers, fuzzy texture, realistic moss, skin detail, ambient occlusion, 3D lighting, reflections and rendering realism.
It must look deliberately illustrated, never Blender/clay/toy/3D.
Keep his slightly asymmetrical face and distinctive ear silhouette instead of replacing him with a generic cute mascot.
Composition: landscape 3:2 art direction sheet on a plain very light cool-gray background.
Three equal full-body drawings across the middle at the same size and baseline, ample separation and padding, no overlap.
Large enough to judge the drawing and face.
Left pose: "Resting", as close as possible to the reference pose and droopy expression.
Middle pose: "Listening", same body and features with a small head/body tilt and eyes glancing sideways, one ear leaning, alert without changing his personality.
Right pose: "Pleased", a small settled smile, relaxed partly closed eyes, subtle raised posture, tooth still visible.
No jumping, confetti or hands.
Text: only the small simple sans serif labels "Resting", "Listening", "Pleased" beneath the respective figures.
No headline, no watermarks, no other text.
Each pose must look like one held frame that could swap instantly to another.
High quality, clean professional illustration.

## Listening revision

Use case: style-transfer.
Asset type: a revised SINGLE Listening pose for existing mascot Bob.
Input image 1 is a character sheet.
Its LEFT "Resting" drawing is the approved identity and drawing style.
Preserve that identity closely.
User feedback: the original MIDDLE Listening pose looked too much like Resting.
Create a much more clearly differentiated Listening pose only.
Do NOT redraw, edit or include the approved Resting drawing.
Do not include Pleased.
ONE character only.
Bob's invariants: broad asymmetrical pear-shaped sage/olive body with pale belly, floppy crooked ear stalks, original eye spacing, small asymmetrical smile and single off-center cream tooth, sparse spots and stylized moss clusters, squat feet and small appendage between them.
NO arms or hands, no new anatomy, accessories or props.
Preserve the thin olive contour and economical flat cel shading from the reference.
Keep colors and character proportions consistent.
No realism, no fur, no 3D.
Listening pose: he is visibly listening toward the viewer's left.
Lean his WHOLE body approximately 12 degrees left, weight clearly on the left foot, the right foot slightly relaxed outward.
One ear is perked distinctly higher and oriented left toward the imagined sound, the other droops naturally; keep both recognizably Bob's floppy bent ear shapes, not pointed rabbit ears.
Both eyes look toward the upper left, eyelids are noticeably lifted compared to the sleepy reference, one eyebrow raised in attentive curiosity.
Mouth is a tiny quiet asymmetrical smile with his one tooth visible, no open surprised mouth.
Expression says "I'm listening", not alarm, sleepiness or generic happiness.
Strong distinct silhouette and expressive eyes that will read at 100 pixels.
Do not simply reuse the resting pose with shifted pupils.
Composition: one full-body drawing centered with generous margins on a plain very light cool gray background, matching the reference sheet.
Square image, figure occupies about 75% of the image height.
Small plain sans-serif label "Listening" centered below.
No sound-wave marks, radiating lines, speech bubbles, UI, additional text or watermark.
Polished illustrated Flash-era character still frame.
